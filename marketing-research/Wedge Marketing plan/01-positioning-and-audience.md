# Positioning & audience

[[Wedge Marketing plan|← Back to plan]]

---

## Primary audience

**Person 2–3 weeks from exam day** who already studied and needs:

- Exam-realistic practice (timed, mixed item types)
- Labs without installing GNS3 or Packet Tracer
- Honest feedback on weak domains
- A short, affordable sprint — not another course

They are **not** shopping for the cheapest question dump. They are asking: *Am I ready?*

---

## Core message

| Layer | Copy |
|-------|------|
| Tagline | Practice like test day. Walk in ready. |
| Wedge headline | Browser CCNA & ENCOR labs — no GNS3. Timed sim. Verified answers. |
| Anti-competitor (internal) | Not a course upsell. Not PDF dumps. Not subscription games. |

---

## Strengths to lead with (in ads, pages, video)

1. **Browser CLI labs** — VLAN, routing concepts, ENCOR ACL/CoPP; no third-party software
2. **120-min timed simulation** — mixed MCQ, drag-and-drop, lab-style pressure
3. **Verified explanations** — Cisco / CompTIA official sources; V_2025 & V_2026 (CCNA)
4. **Review modes** — missed answers loop back; focus weak topics before test day
5. **One-time access** — $9.99 / 10 days or $19.99 / 30 days; progress included in pass
6. **Free try-first** — assessment, samples, VLAN lab — judge quality before checkout

---

## What competitors optimize for (avoid their game)

| Competitor type | Their funnel | Why CPC is high |
|-----------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Course vendors (INE, CBT, Udemy paths) | Free sample → bootcamp | They profit on $500+ LTV |
| Dump / PDF sites | Free teaser → subscription | Volume SEO + low quality |
| Boson-style sims | Desktop install → $100+ product | Brand + install moat |
| ExamTopics / forums | Community recall | Not your customer |

**Do not** compete on “7000+ questions” or “free practice test” alone.

## Personas

### Alex — CCNA, exam in 18 days

- Has watched videos / read OCG; nervous about simlets and drag-drop
- Does not want to install Packet Tracer on work laptop
- Will pay $9.99 for 10 days if timed sim feels real

### Jordan — ENCOR, exam in 3 weeks

- Needs lab sims for ACL, CoPP, routing — not more flashcards
- Comparing Boson ($$$) vs browser option
- Values 8 lab simulations + timed 120-min run

### Sam — Security+ (wedge)

- PBQ / chain labs anxiety; **interactive scenarios like test day**
- **Adaptive review** — weak areas pushed; missed questions shuffle to end
- **Timed online simulation** — not a $99 PDF or desktop install
- **Cert required for work** — DoD, contractor, job mandate
- Browser-no-install · mobile-friendly · **$9.99/10d vs PDF dumps**
- Wedge landing: `/secplus/pbq-practice-browser.html`
- **Campaign docs:** [[../Sec+ Campaign/Sec+ Positioning|Sec+ Positioning]] · [[SEC+ funnel build tracker]]
