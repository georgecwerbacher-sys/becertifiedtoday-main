## Verification rules

### Public explanation

Verification is only for eligibility. It is also not a first-time visitor coupon, homepage promotion, popup, or broad discount CTA.

1. You choose your group.
2. You verify with a school, work, government, military, or partner method.
3. After verification, you get a verified learner discount link or code.
4. At checkout, use the email where you want account and access links. The discount code is tied to the Stripe checkout email.
5. Access links go to the checkout email.

Approved discounts are not one-time only. A verified learner may use the discount again for eligible Be Certified Today products added later, using the same verified checkout email. Approved discount codes should not be shared. Shared codes may fail checkout or be disabled.

### Internal verification map

| Method | Groups |
|--------|--------|
| `.edu` email | Students, educators |
| `.mil` email | Active-duty military, Guard, Reserve |
| `.gov` email | Federal employees, state/local government, first responders where applicable |
| Government-issued contractor mailbox with `.ctr`, `.civ`, or `v-` marker | DoD and federal contractors |
| Approved contractor domain | Government contractors |
| Partner referral code | Workforce programs, veteran programs, transition programs |
| ID.me (future) | Veterans |
| Manual review (future) | Students without `.edu`, veterans without ID.me, workforce learners using personal email |

### Contractor email examples

Government-issued contractor email addresses may use formats like:

```text
[firstname].[lastname].[identifier]@[domain].mil
[firstname].[initial].[lastname].[identifier]@[domain].mil
v-[name]@[agency.gov]
```

Common identifiers:

- `.ctr` for contractor
- `.civ` for civilian
- `v-` for vendor

DoD and federal examples:

- `@mail.mil`
- `@army.mil`
- `@navy.mil`
- `@usmc.mil`
- `@defense.gov`
- `@faa.gov`
- `@energy.gov`
- `@gsa.gov`

**Reject for automated verification:** personal domains such as `gmail.com`, `icloud.com`, `yahoo.com`, or `outlook.com`, unless paired with a valid partner code or manual approval.

### Live v1 form fields

The live form posts to `/api/sample-lead` with `action: "request_question_verification"` and `product: "secplus"`. It reuses the Ask-a-question verification link and admin queue. The structured admin message includes:

- Learner group
- Verification method
- Eligibility email, which must click the verification link
- Discount/access email, where the approved discount code and access setup should be sent
- Organization/program, optional
- Notes, optional
- Page path
- Admin instruction that Stripe discount-code creation remains manual

Client-side checks:

- `.edu`, `.mil`, and `.gov` methods require the matching suffix.
- Government-issued contractor mailbox accepts `.mil`/`.gov` addresses with `.ctr`, `.civ`, or `v-` markers, or non-personal employer domains for review.
- Approved contractor/employer domain rejects common personal email domains.
- Partner/program code and manual review allow personal domains, but copy makes clear the request is reviewed before approval.

## Group-specific copy blocks

### Students

You may need Security+ for a class, capstone, internship, degree requirement, or first IT role. The discount helps you use a current test preparation portal without paying for another full course when you are already studying through school.

### Educators

Educators shape the next group of cybersecurity and IT professionals. A verified educator discount makes it easier to evaluate the same practice environment your students may use: timed simulation, adaptive review, objective-tagged questions, and browser-based PBQs.

### Military

For military learners, Security+ can be tied to MOS requirements, reclassification, promotion paths, DoD baseline expectations, or a transition plan. The product is built for short study windows, mobile use, and deadline-driven prep.

### Veterans

For veterans, certification can be a bridge into federal, contractor, or private-sector cyber roles. The discount recognizes that transition is expensive enough already. You should not have to choose between an overpriced course and a questionable PDF.

### Workforce and job-placement programs

Workforce learners are often preparing for a specific outcome: a placement, apprenticeship, interview, or funded training milestone. The portal supports that with focused practice, timed review, and clear feedback on weak domains.

### First responders

Public-safety work increasingly touches digital evidence, networks, mobile devices, and incident response. First responders and public-safety IT teams need practical, reliable preparation when Security+ is part of the role or training path.

### Federal employees

For federal employees, certification may support a role change, return-to-office requirement, cyber workforce alignment, or agency training goal. The portal gives you a private, browser-based way to test readiness without installing software.

### Government contractors

Contractors often need certification because the contract requires it. The discount can be verified through approved contractor domains or eligible government mailbox patterns.
