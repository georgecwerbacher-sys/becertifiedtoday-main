## Tagline

**Practice Like Test Day. Walk In Ready.**

---

## Why I built this

I built Be Certified Today because most exam prep still asks you to choose between the wrong options: a **static PDF or question dump for $50–$100**, a **desktop simulator you have to install**, or a **$300+ course** when you already know the material and just need to know if you are ready.

I wanted one place where you practice **the way you will be tested**—in your browser, on your phone or laptop, with **interactive scenarios**, **timed simulation**, and **honest feedback** on what still needs work—without a subscription that never ends.

That is true for CCNA, CCNP ENCOR, and CompTIA Security+. Same promise: **exam-realistic practice, verified answers, one clear payment for a defined access period.**

---

## What makes Be Certified Today different

### Interactive scenarios online—like the real exam

Performance-based items are not flat screenshots in a PDF. On Security+, chain labs, drag-and-drop, and IR scenarios run **in the browser** with the same interaction model as full access. On CCNA and ENCOR, you get timed runs, drag-and-drop, and **CLI labs in the browser**—no GNS3, no Packet Tracer install. What you practice is what test day feels like.

### Adaptive prep that pushes what needs work

I do not treat every topic the same. Review modes **prioritize weak areas**. Miss a question in review mode and it **shuffles to the end of the queue** so you try again before you walk in. Run a **random full-bank session** or a **focused review**—your choice, same engine.

### Timed online simulation

A timed exam-style run under real pressure—mixed item types, the pace of the actual certification—not flipping through a dump and hoping you are ready. Security+ includes a **90-minute simulation**; CCNA uses a **120-minute timed run** aligned to exam length.

### A sprint, not a dump—and not a course

Instead of buying a stale PDF for **$99.99**, you can unlock full access for **$9.99 / 10 days** or **$19.99 / 30 days** (Security+ and CCNA; ENCOR primary offer is **$19.99 / 30 days**). **One payment. No membership game.** That includes **1,000+ current-version questions** where applicable, verified explanations, PBQ or lab scenarios, timed sim, and adaptive review in one portal.

### Mobile-friendly practice

Many of you study between shifts, on a commute, or at the kitchen table on a phone. The product is built for **mobile-friendly MCQ and scenario practice**—not desktop-only legacy sims you cannot open unless you are at home.

### Answers I verify—not forum votes

Keys and explanations are checked against **official Cisco and CompTIA sources**, not braindump sites or “most upvoted” recall threads.

### Try before you pay

Free samples use the **same UI and testing engine** as paid access. Judge quality before checkout—no account required for samples.

## Who this is for

- You are **2–3 weeks from test day**, you have already studied, and you are asking: *Am I ready?*
- Your employer, contract, or clearance path **requires the certification**—DoD baseline, contractor role, government mandate, job posting—and you need **realistic prep**, not another bootcamp.
- You were burned by a **PDF dump** or a **random question bank** with no review loop and want **interactive, timed, adaptive practice** instead.

## Who this is not for

- Shoppers optimizing for “most questions per dollar” on a printable dump.
- People starting from zero who need an instructor-led video course—we are **practice and readiness**, not a replacement for learning the material the first time.
