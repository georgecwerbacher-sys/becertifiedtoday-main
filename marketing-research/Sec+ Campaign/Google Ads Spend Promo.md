## Update actuals weekly

1. Google Ads → **Billing → Billing activity** → export CSV (same format)
2. Paste daily **Account spend** into [[google-ads-spend-promo-2026.csv]]
3. Reconcile **Remaining to $500** with Google’s promo **“Check your progress”** widget

**Columns in CSV:**

- **Account spend USD** — all campaign rows (promo basis unless Google says otherwise)
- **Sec+ spend USD** — Security+ / SEC+ campaign names only
- **CCNA wedge USD** / **Other campaigns USD** — split for your records
- **Taxes and fees USD** — usually **not** promo-eligible; tracked separately
