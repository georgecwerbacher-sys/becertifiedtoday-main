## Who requires Security+ (target segments)

### 1. Colleges & universities — students and educators

Programs that treat Security+ as a **graduation, capstone, or internship gate**, plus faculty teaching cyber/IT courses who want the same prep stack as students:

- Cybersecurity AAS/BS tracks (CAE-designated and regional community colleges)
- IT degree paths with **CompTIA stack** (A+ → Network+ → Security+)
- **Students** in programs with published Security+ requirements
- **Educators** — adjunct and full-time faculty, lab instructors, program directors (`.edu` verify)

**Outreach (organic / email — not paid search first):**

- Program chairs, career services, and “required certifications” PDFs on department sites
- **Student & educator discount** via `.edu` verification (see [[#Verified learner discount program (plan)]])
- **Department bulk quote** (future) for cohorts capstoning together
- Start with the larger CAE-designated targets in [[Sec+ CAE College Target List]]

**Geo (Google Ads location targeting):**

- **60-mile radius** around campuses with published Security+ requirements (students + educators)
- College towns: e.g. San Antonio, Colorado Springs, Huntsville, Augusta, Dayton, Pensacola (cyber + military overlap)
- For the `secplus_workforce_us` campaign, use [[Sec+ CAE College Target List#60-mile radius targeting]]

### 2. Career & workforce programs

Organizations that **help people find work** or **retrain into cyber/IT** — often the same candidates who need Security+ before their first role:

| Program type | Examples | Why Security+ |
|--------------|----------|---------------|
| **Federal / state workforce** | WIOA **American Job Centers**, state workforce development boards, unemployment retraining | Funded cert paths toward placed jobs |
| **Military transition** | **SkillBridge**, **TAP**, MOC crosswalk / credentialing counselors | Civilian job often lists Security+ |
| **Veteran employment** | **VR&E**, Hire Heroes USA, VetSec, Operation Code, DAV/ VFW cyber tracks | Cert toward contractor/federal hire |
| **Nonprofit & community retraining** | Goodwill Excel Center, Per Scholas, NPower, Year Up, local cyber bootcamp partners with job placement | Placement tied to cert completion |
| **Apprenticeship & pre-apprenticeship** | Cyber apprenticeship sponsors, union training funds | Employer gate cert before bench |

**Outreach:**

- Partner referral codes for case managers (one code per org; no public listing)
- Allowlist **partner org email domains** + manual review for enrollment letters
- Flyer/one-pager for job centers: “SY0-701 exam prep — verified discount for program participants”

**Not in paid Search RSA** — same separate discount URL as military and `.edu`.

### 3. Military — active duty, Guard, Reserve

Security+ maps to **DoD 8140** baseline and many **MOS / AFSC / rating** cyber-adjacent paths. Candidates often study **on phone between shifts** and need **deadline-driven** prep.

**Geo (US-only paid search):**

- **CONUS:** Use **60-mile radius targeting** around priority installations and base metros: Fort Liberty, JBLM, Fort Cavazos, Fort Moore, Nellis/Creech, Lackland/JBSA, Fort Meade, Norfolk/Hampton Roads, San Diego, Pearl Harbor/Hickam, Scott AFB, MacDill/Tampa, Redstone/Huntsville, Colorado Springs.
- **OCONUS:** Hold for later. This build is **United States only**.

**Channels:**

- r/military, r/securityforces, base Facebook groups (organic — follow sub rules)
- MWR / education office flyers (PDF one-pager linking to cert home + discount page)
- **Do not** imply official DoD endorsement

### 4. Veterans (separate from active duty)

Veterans pursuing **civilian cyber roles**, contractor jobs, or **GI Bill–funded** programs often need Security+ on a fixed timeline after separation.

**Verify with:**

- **ID.me “Verified Veteran”** or similar (future integration)
- `.mil` retiree / `.gov` VA employee email where applicable
- **Workforce partner referral** (SkillBridge alumni, VR&E counselor code, veteran nonprofit partner)
- Manual review: DD-214 + enrollment in an approved employment program (queue — not automated day one)

**Channels:** r/Veterans, r/VeteransBenefits, VetSec, Operation Code, state veteran service officers, American Job Centers with veteran reps.

### 5. Federal civilian & DoD contractors

**8570/8140** baseline roles across agencies (DHS, VA, IRS, GSA, etc.) and **prime/sub contractors** on cleared contracts.

**Geo:**

- National Capital Region (DC, Arlington, Alexandria, Fort Meade corridor)
- Contractor hubs: Huntsville, San Antonio, Colorado Springs, Tampa, Omaha, Charleston SC
- **Return-to-office density:** prioritize metros with high concentrations of federal employees and contractors commuting back into offices: DC/Northern Virginia/Maryland, Fort Meade corridor, Huntsville, San Antonio, Colorado Springs, Tampa, Norfolk/Hampton Roads, San Diego, Omaha, Charleston SC, and large agency regional-office cities.
- Avoid rural federal footprint for now unless tied to a known installation, agency campus, or contractor hub.

**First federal/DoD city pass:** Washington, D.C.; Arlington, VA; Alexandria, VA; San Diego, CA; San Antonio, TX; Norfolk, VA; Honolulu, HI; Huntsville, AL; Colorado Springs, CO; Denver, CO; Oklahoma City, OK; Jacksonville, FL; El Paso, TX; Atlanta, GA.

**Message:** Exam prep aligned to **SY0-701** with **objective-tagged, Tier A–verified** items — not brain dumps. Link [[how-we-verify-questions.html]] when live.

### 6. First responders & state/local government

Many **state cyber grants** and **fusion center** training lists include Security+. Police/fire/EMS IT and digital forensics adjunct roles.

**Geo:** Target **state capitals and larger population centers** first. Avoid rural counties, small towns, and broad low-density regions until metro campaigns show checkout signal. Use large county seats only when they overlap public-safety, state/local government, or metro IT hiring.

**Verification:** State/county `.gov` email or approved domains list (see below). Some agencies use `.us` or third-party email — manual review queue for edge cases.

### 7. Government contractors (non-.gov email)

Use **employer domain allowlist** (e.g. `@lockheedmartin.com`, `@leidos.com`) or approved government-issued contractor email patterns. When a contractor is provided direct access to DoD or federal agency networks, their email address often includes identifiers such as `.ctr`, `.civ`, or `v-`.

**Common layouts:**

- `[firstname].[lastname].[identifier]@[domain].mil`
- `[firstname].[initial].[lastname].[identifier]@[domain].mil`
- Vendor-style prefix such as `v-[name]@[agency.gov]`

**Common identifiers:** `.ctr` (contractor), `.civ` (civilian), `v-` (vendor).

**DoD domain examples:** `@mail.mil`, `@army.mil`, `@navy.mil`, `@usmc.mil`, `@defense.gov`.

**Federal civilian examples:** `@faa.gov`, `@energy.gov`, `@gsa.gov`.

Treat these addresses as **eligibility verification only**. Account and access links go to the email used at checkout.

If the email does not match `.gov`, `.mil`, a known contractor/civilian/vendor marker, or an approved contractor domain, route to manual review.

### Workforce partner model (referral codes)

For programs that aid job seekers, email domains vary and some clients only have personal email:

1. **Partner onboarding** — spreadsheet of org name, contact, allowed email domains, optional flat referral code (e.g. `WORKFORCE-AJC-STATEXX`)
2. **Case manager** gives learner the code or verifies learner on org email
3. Learner completes verify step → Stripe promo → checkout with the email they want to use for account and access links
4. Track redemptions per partner in Stripe metadata until admin segment exists

**Starter partner targets:** American Job Center network (by state), SkillBridge coordinators, VR&E counselors, VetSec / Operation Code chapters, Per Scholas / NPower / Year Up where Security+ appears in placement tracks.

## Eligibility email verification — rules

**Principle:** School, work, or program email proves eligibility. Checkout uses the email where the learner wants account and access links. Implementation details live in [[../Website_Main/Email Verification Setup|Email Verification Setup]].

### Flow

1. User opens **verified discount** page from organic, email, QR, job center flyer, campus link, or partner referral (not paid Search RSA, first-visitor popup, homepage banner, or generic promo CTA).
2. User selects category: **Student · Educator · Military · Veteran · Workforce program · First responder · Federal · Contractor**.
3. User verifies eligibility:
   - **`.edu`** — student or educator (same domain check; role selected on form)
  - **`.mil` / `.gov`** — military, federal, public service, or government-issued contractor address
   - **Partner referral code** — workforce, veteran employment, or transition programs
   - **ID.me Verified Veteran** (future)
4. System sends **one-time verification link** to the school/work email **or** validates referral code / ID.me.
5. After success, user receives the appropriate verified learner Stripe promotion code or pre-filled checkout link.
6. User completes Stripe checkout with the email they want to use for access. The discount is tied to that verified checkout email, is not one-time only, and can be used again for eligible Be Certified Today products added later.
7. Access links for `/COMP_TIA_SEC+/SEC+_Training_Portal.html` go to the checkout email, same as today (`secplus-portal-request-link.html`).

**Code rule:** Approved discount codes are for the verified learner only. Do not share them publicly or treat them as first-visitor coupons.

**Students without `.edu` mail:** Manual queue — enrollment letter or student ID upload (Phase 2b). Do not require `.edu` as portal email.

### Approved domains & codes (starter — expand in repo config)

| Pattern / method | Segment |
|------------------|---------|
| `*.edu` | Students & educators |
| `*.mil` | Active-duty military |
| `*.gov` | Federal / many state agencies |
| `*.state.*.us`, county/city `.gov` | First responders / local gov |
| `.mil` / `.gov` mailbox with contractor, civilian, or vendor marker (`.ctr`, `.civ`, `v-`, etc.) | DoD / federal contractors and government-issued contractor mailboxes |
| Contractor allowlist CSV | Defense contractors |
| Workforce partner allowlist + referral codes | Job centers, veteran employment orgs, retraining nonprofits |
| ID.me Verified Veteran | Veterans (no `.mil` required) |

**Reject for automated verify:** Personal domains (`gmail.com`, `yahoo.com`, `icloud.com`, `outlook.com`) unless paired with valid **partner referral code** or manual approval.

### Abuse controls

- Rate-limit verification sends per IP and per domain
- Log verifications; revoke codes if Stripe fraud signals fire
- Manual review queue: students without `.edu`, workforce clients with only personal email, veteran DD-214 uploads
- **Educator `.edu` shares student domain** — rely on role attestation + rate limits; escalate obvious abuse
