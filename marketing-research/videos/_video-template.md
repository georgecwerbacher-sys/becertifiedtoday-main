## OBS — record checklist

- [ ] Scene **A-roll**: cam 1080p · mic −12 to −6 dB peaks · light key 45°
- [ ] Scene **B-roll screen**: 1920×1080 · browser 125% zoom · hide bookmarks bar
- [ ] Scene **B-roll portal**: sample URL only · no paid bank · no personal email
- [ ] Record **A-roll and screen separately** (easier jump cuts in Premiere)
- [ ] Clap or snap at start of each take (sync marker)
