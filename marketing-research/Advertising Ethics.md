# Advertising ethics

Be Certified Today sells **legitimate exam prep**. Ads must not target, imply, or compete with unauthorized exam content.

Related: [[Writing Guidelines]] · [[Site Mission]] · [[Campaigns]]

---

## Policy (hard)

**Do not bid on** dump, brain dump, exam dump, cheat, cheat sheet, actual exam, real exam questions, pass guaranteed, VCE/ETE dump-player, or torrent intent.

**Do not run** a dump-intercept campaign. Intercepting people who search for dumps still associates the brand with cheating, even if the RSA never says “dump.”

**Do not claim** that practice items are harvested from PDFs, forums, study groups, or dump answer keys. Keys are verified on official / Tier A sources. Guest copy leads with that.

**Do add** those dump and cheat terms as **campaign negatives** on every live Search campaign.

---

## Google Ads ops (do this in the account)

1. Pause or remove `Security+ SY0-701 · US Search · Dump Intercept` if it exists.
2. On `Security+ SY0-701 · US Search · Launch 40`, paste campaign negatives from [[Sec+ Campaign/Sec+ Launch 40 Keyword Lists]].
3. Search terms: any dump / cheat / actual-exam query → negative, never a positive keyword.
4. RSA: do not use “dump,” “braindump,” “Not-A-Dump,” or “Not a PDF dump.”

---

## Allowed contrast

Compare against **static PDF packs**, **desktop test engines**, and **video courses**. Do not name dump marketplaces or use dump as an SEO hook.

Never in ads or paid landings: actual exam questions, real exam, dumps, pass guaranteed, competitor brand names.
